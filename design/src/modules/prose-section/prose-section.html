01 · Problem

A hidden ledger of ownership

Every used car carries a paper trail — service records, accident reports, title transfers — and almost none of it survives the sale. Buyers inherit the asset without the history, and sellers walk away with the story.

The team's early user research surfaced a single shared complaint: nobody trusts the previous owner. Not because they were lying, but because there was no shared record to trust. We wanted to build one.
